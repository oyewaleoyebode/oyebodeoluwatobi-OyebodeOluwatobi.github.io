I am pleased to share that our paper, **“Speaker Identification for Low-End Devices: A Secure Voice Biometric Solution for Mobile Banking,”** has been published in the proceedings of the **2025 11th IEEE International Conference on Privacy Computing and Data Security (PCDS)**.

This publication represents an important stage in my research on secure and accessible voice-biometric authentication.

## Why we conducted this research

Mobile banking has significantly expanded access to financial services. However, many users still depend primarily on passwords, PINs, and other knowledge-based credentials that can be stolen, disclosed, guessed, or shared.

The problem is particularly important for people who rely on low-end mobile devices without dependable fingerprint scanners, facial-recognition systems, or other dedicated biometric capabilities. We therefore investigated whether a person’s voice could provide an additional layer of identity verification in such environments.

## Our approach

We developed a speaker-identification system that combines representations derived from **Whisper** and **ECAPA-TDNN**. These models provide complementary information about speech and speaker identity.

The system was evaluated using recordings collected across multiple devices and recording environments. We also incorporated a liveness-detection component to strengthen the system against the non-live voice samples represented in our experiments.

The study demonstrates the potential of voice biometrics as an accessible authentication mechanism for mobile-banking environments involving low-end devices and variable recording conditions.

## Looking beyond this work

This research provided the foundation for my subsequent work on:

- Speaker verification under acoustic degradation
- Cancellable speaker credentials
- Biometric-template protection
- Homomorphic encrypted verification
- Privacy leakage in protected speaker representations

I am grateful to my co-authors, **Yuzo Taenaka** and **Youki Kadobayashi**, for their contributions and guidance throughout this work.
