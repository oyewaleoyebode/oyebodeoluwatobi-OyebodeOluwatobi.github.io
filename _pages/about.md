Welcome to my personal academic website! 

I am a PhD Candidate in Cybersecurity at the **Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST)** in Japan. My research focuses on cyber resilience, voice biometrics, and secure communication systems.

---

### Research Interests
* **Voice Biometrics & Audio Security:** Synthetic voice detection, deepfake audio defense, and biometric authentication.
* **Cyber Resilience:** System security, threat modeling, and intrusion mitigation.
* **Network & Systems Security:** Protocols, access control, and privacy-preserving frameworks.
